Craftsmanship

An hour of the hand
in every link.

Nothing here is mass made. Chains are drawn, soldered and polished by hand, then weighed against the light until they fall exactly as they should across the back of the wrist.

  • I Solid 18k gold & 925 sterling, never plated to fade.
  • II The cross is hand set, never glued.
  • III Adjustable drape, fitted to your hand.
  • IV Presented in a signature cream keepsake box.
